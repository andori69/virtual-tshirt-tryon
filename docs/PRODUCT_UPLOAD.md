# Adding and publishing a T-shirt

1. **Owner → Add New T-shirt.** Enter the name and price, and optionally brand, description and currency. Press **Save as Draft**.
2. **Sizes & measurements.** Tick each size you sell and enter real measurements of your stock, in cm. See the guide below.
3. **Colors.** Add a color, for example "Navy". Each color gets its own photo and stock.
4. **Photo.** In the color card, choose **Upload photo** (drag and drop or file picker; PNG, JPG or WEBP up to 15 MB) or **Use camera** (pick a camera, Capture, Retake if needed). Press **Confirm & process**. The background is removed on this computer in a few seconds.
5. **Review.** The original and the processed cut-out appear side by side.
   - If processing failed, the reason is shown. Retake the photo, or press **Retry processing**.
   - Use **⟲/⟳ 2°**, **Crop** (drag a box to keep) and **Undo rotate/crop** to tidy the result.
6. **Anchor points.** Drag each dot onto the garment, or focus a dot and use the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps). Hover a dot to see its name. Left and right mean the left and right of the picture. Then press **Confirm anchors**. A new photo, rotation or crop needs re-confirming.
7. **Stock.** Enter a quantity per size and press **Save stock**. 0 means sold out; customers can't pick that size.
8. **Preview Virtual Garment** opens the camera with landmarks drawn, so you can check alignment on yourself.
9. **Publish Product.** The button is enabled once the checklist in the Publishing panel is clear. Drafts and archived products never appear on the kiosk.

The original photo is always kept. **Retry processing** regenerates every derived file from it.

## Photographing

- Photograph the front of the shirt, with the whole garment in frame, sleeves and hem included.
- Lay it flat or hang it straight, and smooth out folds.
- Use a plain background that contrasts with the shirt. A white shirt needs a dark background.
- Use even, bright light. Avoid strong shadows and anything covering the shirt.
- Hold the camera level, pointing straight at the garment.

## Measurement guide (all in cm, measured flat)

| Measurement | How |
|---|---|
| Chest width | Straight across from armpit to armpit. This is **half** the shirt's circumference, not a body chest size. |
| Shoulder width | Seam to seam across the back. |
| Body length | From the high point of the shoulder, beside the collar, to the hem. |
| Sleeve length | From the shoulder seam to the sleeve end. |
| Sleeve opening (optional) | Straight across the sleeve end. |

Measure your own stock. Brands differ, so don't copy a generic chart. Allowed ranges can be changed in **Settings → Allowed measurement range**.

## Managing products

- **Unpublish** returns a product to draft and hides it from customers.
- **Archive** hides it and keeps its record. **Restore as draft** brings it back.
- **Delete permanently** removes the product and all its files.
- **Inventory** lists every color × size with low-stock and sold-out filters, inline edits and **Mark sold out**.
